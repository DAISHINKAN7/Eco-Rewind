## 2. RESEARCH FRAMING (from `docs/PLAN.md`)

Question: *Can a model trained only on pre-disturbance satellite time series produce counterfactual trajectories reliable
enough to attribute post-hurricane change, and how much does the attribution depend on the choice of model?*

| Claim | Test |
|---|---|
| C1. The counterfactual is accurate where no disturbance happened | Skill on held-out spatial blocks and placebo dates vs classical baselines |
| C2. The counterfactual gap detects real damage | Placebo gaps ≈ 0; agreement with independent damage evidence |
| C3. Attribution is sensitive to model choice | Spread of attributed loss across 8+ models with calibrated intervals |

Carbon is **illustrative only** (published carbon densities with ranges), never a core claim.
Each claim stands alone; the week-6 gate (Phase 4) decides whether the paper is a method, benchmark, or cautionary paper.

## 3. NON-NEGOTIABLE INTEGRITY RULES

1. **Temporal cutoff:** a counterfactual model for an event (real or placebo) never sees any quarter at or after that
   event's landfall date — not in training, normalisation, early stopping, or model selection.
2. **Spatial isolation:** no pixel is in two folds; buffers between folds are excluded from evaluation;
   normalisation statistics come only from training blocks.
3. **Masking:** invalid pixels never contribute to a loss, metric, normalisation statistic or attribution sum.
4. **Pre-declared criteria:** placebo pass criteria, sweep grids and kill criteria are committed to
   `configs/preregistration.yaml` **before** the runs they govern. Commit hash recorded in the report.
5. **Model selection on validation R² per band**, never on loss alone (v1 lesson: loss could not detect a degenerate predictor).
6. **Report every model that was run**, including failures, in an appendix table.

## 5. PHASE 0 — RE-EXPORT FROM EARTH ENGINE (CPU / free; gate for everything)

Write the export with the **Earth Engine Python API** (runnable from Colab CPU), exporting **Cloud-Optimised GeoTIFFs
to Google Drive**. Commit the scripts. Verify every collection ID and band name against the current Earth Engine catalogue
before use and record them in `docs/DATA.md` — the IDs below are the expected ones, not guaranteed.

### 5.1 Sites, grids, calendar (`configs/sites.yaml`, `configs/tracks.yaml`)
- AOIs = the bounds in Section 1 (optionally buffer 1 km for edge context; evaluation stays inside the original AOI).
- CRS: **Everglades EPSG:32617 (UTM 17N); Mississippi EPSG:32615 (UTM 15N)**.
- **One fixed grid per site per track**: Track A 10 m, Track B 30 m, with the 30 m grid origin aligned to the 10 m grid
  (30 = 3 × 10) so the two tracks nest exactly. Same grid for every quarter (pass `crs` and `crsTransform` explicitly).
- Calendar quarters (Jan–Mar = Q1 …).

### 5.2 Track A — Sentinel-2, 10 m, 2015Q3 → 2023Q4
- **Check which Sentinel-2 product exists over each AOI for the whole period.** If surface reflectance
  (`COPERNICUS/S2_SR_HARMONIZED`) does not cover 2015Q3–2016 over an AOI, use **top-of-atmosphere harmonized
  (`COPERNICUS/S2_HARMONIZED`) for the entire series of that site** — consistency over correction. Never mix products
  within a series. Document the choice per site.
- Bands: B2, B3, B4, B8, B11, B12 (B11/B12 resampled to 10 m, bilinear). Keep DN scale (0–10000); record scale factor.
- **Cloud masking per pixel with Cloud Score+** (`GOOGLE/CLOUD_SCORE_PLUS/V1/S2_HARMONIZED`, threshold `cs_cdf` ≥ 0.6 as a
  starting point; make it a config value). **No scene-level cloud filter.**
- Composite = per-pixel **median** of clear observations within the quarter.
- Extra bands: `n_clear_obs` (count of clear observations per pixel), `doy_mean` (mean day-of-year of contributing observations).
- Do **not** export NDVI/NDWI; compute them locally from bands (removes the −1 fill bug).

### 5.3 Track B — HLS, 30 m, 2013Q2 → 2023Q4
- Collections: `NASA/HLS/HLSL30/v002` (Landsat 8/9) and `NASA/HLS/HLSS30/v002` (Sentinel-2), merged.
- Common bands: Blue, Green, Red, NIR (narrow), SWIR1, SWIR2. Mask with the HLS **Fmask** band (cloud, cloud shadow,
  adjacent cloud, snow, water-flag kept as a separate output, not masked).
- Same median compositing and `n_clear_obs`, `doy_mean` bands.
- Irma then has ~17 pre-event quarters (2013Q2–2017Q2); Ida ~33.

### 5.4 Sentinel-1 — for both tracks (fixes the SAR bug)
- `COPERNICUS/S1_GRD`, filter **`instrumentMode == 'IW'`**, **both orbit directions**, polarisations **VV and VH** present.
  Do not filter on relative orbit or platform.
- Convert to linear power, per-pixel **mean in linear units** per quarter (reduces speckle), convert back to dB.
  Optionally a 3×3 focal median before compositing (config flag).
- Bands `VV`, `VH`, `VH_VV_ratio`, `n_s1_obs`. Export on both track grids.
- S1 starts Oct 2014 (S1A); S1B failed Dec 2021 (fewer acquisitions after). Quarters with `n_s1_obs == 0` → SAR mask.
- **Sanity check:** expected `n_s1_obs` per quarter ≈ 6–15 over these AOIs before 2022. If most quarters are 0, the filter is still wrong — stop and report.

### 5.5 Event-quarter handling (`configs/events.yaml`)
```yaml
irma: {site: everglades, landfall: 2017-09-10, event_quarter: 2017Q3, first_full_post: 2017Q4}
ida:  {site: mississippi, landfall: 2021-08-29, event_quarter: 2021Q3, first_full_post: 2021Q4}
```
- Export the event quarter as two partial composites: `PRE` (quarter start → landfall − 1 day) and
  `POST` (landfall + 3 days → quarter end). Store them separately; the regular quarterly series has **no** value for the
  event quarter.
- Counterfactual inputs end at the last full pre-event quarter (optionally + the `PRE` partial as an extra input, ablated).
- Evaluation and attribution start at `first_full_post`.

### 5.6 Static layers (exported once per site per track grid)
- Copernicus DEM GLO-30 elevation; distance to coastline (computed); **ESA WorldCover** land cover (10 m) for the
  ecosystem mask. Note: WorldCover maps are 2020/2021, i.e. post-Irma; use only to exclude permanent water/urban/cropland,

- Wetland evaluation mask = WorldCover "herbaceous wetland" + "mangroves" (+ "tree cover" inside the Everglades AOI if it

### 5.7 Naming and verification

## 6. PHASE 1 — DATA CUBE, MASKS, BLOCKS, FOLDS (CPU)

1. **Cube:** per site per track, a Zarr array `(T, C, H, W)` float32 + validity mask `(T, C, H, W)` bool + `n_clear_obs`,
   chunked by spatial tile. Channels: bands, locally computed NDVI, NDWI (McFeeters, Green/NIR) and NDMI (NIR/SWIR1),
   VV, VH, VH/VV.
2. **Validity rules (`masks.py`):** optical invalid where any reflectance band is 0, NaN, saturated, or `n_clear_obs == 0`;
   SAR invalid where `n_s1_obs == 0` or non-finite; derived indices invalid wherever an input band is invalid or the
   denominator is ~0. Optional confidence weight from `n_clear_obs` (ablated).
3. **Normalisation:** per-channel robust statistics (median/IQR) from **training blocks and pre-event quarters only**,
   per fold, saved as JSON with the data hash. Indices stay in physical units.
4. **Gap filling for model input only:** fill invalid inputs with the pixel's pre-event seasonal climatology from training
   data, and always pass the mask as an input channel. Never fill targets.
5. **Static channels:** elevation, distance to coast, land-cover one-hot.
6. **Spatial blocks:** 2.56 km × 2.56 km blocks (256 px at 10 m; ~85 px at 30 m, same metric footprint in both tracks).
   **5 folds per site**, stratified by wetland fraction and by a damage-exposure proxy (distance to storm track or a
   simple NDVI-drop proxy computed only for stratification, documented). **Buffer of one patch width** between blocks
   of different folds, excluded from evaluation. Folds are identical across tracks (same blocks).
7. **Patch sampling:** Track A 128 × 128, stride 128 within training blocks; Track B 64 × 64 (1.92 km), stride 64.
   Report patch counts per fold.
8. **Placebo windows (pre-declared in `configs/preregistration.yaml`):**
   - Ida (Mississippi): fake landfalls 2018-08-29 and 2019-08-29 (Track A and B), optional 2020-08-29 is **excluded**
     (2020 was an active Louisiana hurricane season — check and document whether a real storm affected the AOI).
   - Irma (Everglades, Track B): fake landfalls 2015-09-10 and 2016-09-10 (Track B only; Track A history too short).
   - Before finalising, check each placebo window against the hurricane record (e.g. NOAA historical tracks) and replace any
     window where a real storm affected the AOI.
9. **Tests (Section 12) for leakage, masking and cutoffs must pass before Phase 2.**

**Gate:** cubes built, folds visualised (`figures/folds_{site}.png`), all Phase-1 tests green. Stop and report.

## 7. PHASE 2 — EVALUATION HARNESS + CPU BASELINES

Build the **harness once**; every model (classical or deep) outputs predictions in the same format:
`(site, track, fold, seed, event_or_placebo, quarter, H, W, C, {q10, q50, q90})` saved as Zarr.

### 7.1 Metrics (`eval/metrics.py`)
Per band, per site, per fold, per horizon, on valid evaluation-mask pixels only: R², RMSE, MAE, bias; plus interval
coverage and mean width (90%). Aggregate as mean ± std over folds and seeds. Primary bands for claims: **NDVI, NDMI, NDWI**.

### 7.2 Conformal calibration (`eval/conformal.py`)
Split conformal (and conformalised quantile regression for quantile models) using a **calibration block set** held out
inside each training fold. Report empirical coverage on test blocks and on placebo windows.

### 7.3 Attribution (`eval/attribution.py`)
Attributed loss = Σ over valid wetland pixels and post-event quarters of (counterfactual − observed), for NDVI/NDMI,
area-weighted, with intervals propagated from conformal bounds (and, separately, from seed ensembles).
Report per site, per horizon (Q+1, Q+2, Q+4, Q+8) and cumulative. Also a **per-pixel attribution map**.

### 7.4 CPU baselines (`models/baselines/`)
1. **Persistence** — last valid full pre-event quarter.
2. **Seasonal climatology** — per-pixel, per-calendar-quarter mean of training quarters.
3. **Harmonic regression (CCDC-style)** — per pixel: intercept + linear trend + 1–2 annual harmonics, fitted on
   pre-event valid observations (robust fit, e.g. Huber); forecast forward; residual-based intervals.
4. **Synthetic control / matched difference-in-differences** — donor pool = pixels in blocks with low damage-exposure proxy;
   match on pre-event trajectory; report as a region-level counterfactual (pixel-level where stable).
5. **LightGBM** — per-pixel features: last k quarters of each channel, quarter-of-year, static channels, neighbourhood
   means (3×3, 7×7); quantile objectives for q10/q50/q90; direct multi-horizon (one model per horizon or horizon as a feature).

**Gate:** baseline table on all folds for both tracks; placebo runs for baselines; conformal coverage reported. Stop and report.

## 8. PHASE 3 — DEEP MODELS (GPU: Colab T4 default)

Common contract: input = `T_in` past quarters × channels (+ masks + static), output = direct multi-horizon quantiles

schedule; early stopping and checkpoint selection on **validation NDVI R²** (computed every N epochs);
**kill criterion: abort if validation NDVI R² < 0 after 15 epochs** (pre-registered).

`T_in`: Track A Ida 8 quarters; Track B Irma 8 quarters; Track B Ida 8 (ablate 4 and 12). `H`: up to 8 quarters.

| Model | Notes |
|---|---|
| ConvLSTM | v1 continuity baseline, kept small |
| SimVP (or TAU) | Simple, strong spatiotemporal predictor; implement from the paper or a vetted MIT/Apache repo (check licence) |
| **EcoTransformer v2** | Your model, redesigned: (1) direct multi-horizon output (no autoregressive rollout); (2) mask + static channels as inputs; (3) 8-quarter input; (4) quantile heads; (5) **SAR gating** of optical features retained but ablated; (6) loss = masked pinball first; SSIM/smoothness terms only if an ablation shows gains; (7) learned quarter-of-year embedding |
| Presto-based head | Pretrained pixel-time-series encoder for S1+S2; fine-tune a light head; works on Track A (and Track B if band mapping is valid — check). Verify current checkpoint and licence |
| *(Expanded scope)* Earthformer | Cuboid-attention Earth-system forecaster; L4 if memory requires |

## 9. PHASE 4 — PLACEBO TESTS (the deciding experiment)

For every model (baselines + deep), for every pre-declared placebo window: retrain with the cutoff at the fake landfall
(same hyperparameters, no retuning), forecast, compute attributed loss with intervals.

- **Placebo in time:** attributed loss should be ≈ 0 and inside the model's interval.
- **Placebo in space:** after the real event, in blocks with the lowest damage-exposure proxy, the gap should be ≈ 0.
- **Pass criterion (pre-registered):** real-event attributed loss exceeds the 95th percentile of the model's placebo
  distribution (placebo windows × folds × seeds), and placebo coverage of the 90% interval ≥ 80%.

Produce `reports/placebo_verdict.md` with a per-model pass/fail table, then **stop**. I choose the paper framing:
- deep models pass and beat baselines → **method paper**;
- baselines match/beat deep models and pass → **benchmark paper**;
- most models fail → **cautionary paper** (attribution is model-dependent and often unfalsifiable; here is a protocol).

## 12. TESTS (pytest; CI on GitHub Actions; ≥ 85% coverage on `src/` excluding plotting)

- **Leakage:** no pixel in two folds; buffers excluded; normalisation stats computed only from training blocks and
  pre-cutoff quarters (assert via recorded indices).
- **Temporal cutoff:** for random events/placebos, perturb all data at/after the cutoff and assert model inputs, normalisation,
  and early-stopping metrics are unchanged.
- **Masking:** set invalid pixels to extreme values; assert losses, metrics and attribution are unchanged.
- **Indices:** NDVI/NDWI/NDMI match hand-computed values; zero/invalid inputs never produce −1 or ±inf.
- **Grid:** all quarters share CRS/transform/shape; Track A/B grids nest (30 m pixel = 3×3 10 m pixels).
- **SAR:** linear-mean-then-dB conversion correct on fixtures; `n_s1_obs == 0` ⇒ masked.
- **Conformal:** coverage ≈ nominal on synthetic data with known noise.
- **Checkpoint resume:** kill-and-resume yields identical metrics to an uninterrupted run (CPU, deterministic mode).
- **Smoke:** every model trains 1 epoch on 10 patches on CPU in CI.
